GoreeCloud YouTube Player is a development-stage, open-source YouTube and YouTube Music client.

It is derived from the NouTube project under the AGPL-3.0 license. Source availability, provenance and acknowledgements are documented in the GoreeCloud repository.

The codebase contains local video, music and library features, but native Android and iOS runtime acceptance, privacy testing, accessibility and release signing are still in progress. GoreeCloud cloud sync, accounts and payments are not connected.

This text documents a development candidate. It is not a released app listing, compatibility guarantee or a download link.

Source: https://github.com/GoreeCloud/youtube-player
